What changes Adding is a labelled toolbar on top of the map, with each shortcut key in its tooltip, not five small icons in a side rail.Every tool says what to do next. While drawing, the hint shows the stop count with Finish and Undo stop.Bus route is its own tool: it starts a route already set to bus service.The right panel opens on what you selected, or a getting-started checklist. Analysis tools move to an Analyse tab and map styles to Style.Search keeps its regex builder, one tap away beside the box.On a phone the tools are a bottom bar that is always visible, and routes, selection and analysis sit in a sheet above it.Nothing is removed: every existing tool, panel and shortcut is still there.
